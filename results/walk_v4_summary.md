# Walk v4: summary

Hand-written summary of `results/walk_v4.md` (generated by `scripts/walk_v4.py eval`).
Per-event detail: `results/sanpo/walk_v4_events.md`.

## Identity scorer (`scripts/score_walk_id.py`)

- Each perception box is matched to a SANPO instance mask by rect IoU on the 320x180
  grid (box pixels vs mask pixels, best instance, IoU >= 0.25). Masks are 2208x1242,
  clips 1280x720 scaled from the same frames, boxes normalised: same 16:9 frame, so
  no conversion beyond scaling. Checked: median best IoU peaks at frame offset 0 in
  all 11 clips (0.24 to 0.54), and drops to 0.00 to 0.18 with mirrored boxes.
- A must_warn event's tracks are those assigned to one of its instances (merged
  crowd events keep all members) in at least 2 frames between earliest - 1 s and
  t_close + 0.5 s. All 33 events have at least one matched track.
- A word or tone counts only if its triggering track is one of those, it starts no
  earlier than the class scorer's earliest time and it ends by that member's t_close.
  Side is reported three ways: first credited cue, last on-time cue about the event,
  any cue.
- Precision: warning's track is an instance that comes within 5 m in the 1.0 m
  corridor, warning starting 5 s before to 1 s after that instance's nearest point.
- Not fooled by talking often: shifted 7 s or 13 s, every system scores 0 or 1 of 33
  (class scorer on the same shifted runs: v2 22 to 24 of 33). Swapping each warning's
  track for a random other near person in the same frame: v2 9.8 (real 15), v3 cues
  13.2 (real 24). Random near people in a crowd are often the ones who pass next, so
  this is the harder baseline. Varying IoU 0.15 to 0.35 and 1 to 3 frames moves
  on-time counts by 2 at most.
- Internet clips have no masks: class-based strict scorer there, as before.

## Side rules

- The internet clips could not separate the rules (cues side right 9 or 10 of 29 for
  all). I picked "metric X at contact over the full TTC" by reasoning. On SANPO it
  was worse than v3's rule (9 vs 11 first cue), so it is not recommended.
- No rule clearly beats v3 or v2 on SANPO: first-cue side right 8 to 14 of 33. The
  best, box centre with no "ahead" (14/33), gains from the labels (1 of 33 is
  "ahead") and makes the most left/right swaps on the person's own box (37/165 vs
  22/165 for the v2 rule). Split by clip (A = 6 clips, 19 events; B = 5 clips, 14
  events): the best rules on A (5/19) score 5 to 7 of 14 on B, below v3's 8; the best
  on B (box centre, no ahead, 10/14) scores 4/19 on A against v3's 3/19.
- The limit is timing, not the rule. Applying each rule to the labelled person's
  own box (all rules except world drift, which is worst everywhere): 22 to 29 of 31
  right 0.5 s before t_close, 19 to 24 of 29 at 1.5 s, 12 to 16 of 26 at 2.5 s, 9 to
  12 of 20 at 3 s. Cues fire 2 to 3 s out, where the side is
  close to a coin flip. Side of the last cue (v3 15/33) beats the first (11/33)
  because repeat tones re-pan as the person comes close.

Recommended: keep v3 BASE, including its side rule (`RECOMMENDED` in
`scripts/walk_v4.py`). Next step, not tested: commit the side only under about 1.5 s
TTC (an unpanned or centred first tone, then panned repeats), scored by last-cue side.

## Caveats

- SANPO labels are automatic (depth plus mostly machine-propagated masks) and all 33
  events are people passing 0.7 to 1.0 m to the side; no vehicles.
- Crowd events merge up to 4 people, so a warning about any member counts.
- 33 events: differences of 1 to 3 are within noise.
- v3 and v4 thresholds other than the side rule were tuned on the internet clips only.
