# Walk v5: summary

Hand-written summary of `results/walk_v5.md` (generated by `scripts/walk_v5.py eval`).

v5 holds the side back: tones are centred ("something is coming", side "none")
until the track's urgency is under the commit threshold, a panned tone plays when
it commits, and words (which name a side) wait until then.

## Result: worse than v3 on both sets

- Threshold picked on the internet clips before SANPO: cues on time with side right
  were 2, 3 and 4 of 29 at 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 s (v3 BASE 10), so 2.0 s was picked.
- SANPO, identity scorer, 33 must_warn, v5 at 2.0 s against v3 BASE:
  - on time, any cue: 24 vs 24
  - side right at the first side-bearing cue: 7 vs 11
  - side right at the last cue: 6 vs 15
  - words on time: 6 vs 8
  - per minute: 14.3 utterances and 25.8 tones, vs 17.4 and 23.1
- Panning the first tone when the track is already under 2.0 s raises v5 to 10 (first
  side-bearing) and 12 (last cue), still below v3.
- Shifted 7 s: 0/33 for both, so the scorer still holds.

## Why

In the v5 2.0 s run, 23 of the 33 events have a matched track under 2.0 s urgency,
most with 1 to 2.5 s to spare, but only 10 get any side-bearing cue on time. The
side-bearing cues are lost in the queue:

- 197 centred "new" tones were queued, 104 of them played. With the 0.85 s tone gap
  they keep the tone channel busy.
- Of the panned cues, only 7 commit tones were queued (4 went stale). Panned repeats
  played 20 times, and 11 more went stale.
- A track that commits under 1.5 s usually takes the word path instead of a commit
  tone, and a 0.7 s word must still end before the person passes.

v3 gives an early, often wrong side, and later repeats correct it. v5 gives no side
until a slot that is usually already taken.

## Recommendation

Keep v3 BASE. If this is tried again, give committed (panned) cues priority over
centred ones on the tone channel, or drop centred repeats. Neither is tested.

## Caveats

Same as `results/walk_v4_summary.md`: automatic SANPO labels, all people, merged
crowd events, and 33 events, so differences of 1 to 3 are noise. The internet-clip
pick favoured the least delay, and none of the three thresholds came near v3.
